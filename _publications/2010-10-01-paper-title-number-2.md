paperurl: 'https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21324'
excerpt: 'In this paper we prove that the pinched Veronese subalgebra has no Koszul filtration regardless of the field k, despite it being a Koszul algebra. We do this by showing that no ideal minimally generated by two linear forms can belong to a Koszul filtration. By the definition of a Koszul filtration this, in turn, implies that no Koszul filtration can exist.'
